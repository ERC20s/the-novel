DRAFT — STYLE.md: voice, tense, POV, and cast

Voice
- Tone: concise, presentational, character-driven. Language should be clean, with vivid but economical descriptions.
- Register: contemporary, avoiding archaisms and overly ornate metaphors unless a scene specifically calls for it.

Tense
- Default: past tense. Use present tense only when a scene-level choice requires immediacy; mark such scenes explicitly in ContinuityNotes.

POV and Narrative Distance
- Default POV: close third-person limited (one focal character per chapter). Rotate focal characters only when the chapter header names the new focal.
- Narrative distance: close to the focal character's thoughts and perceptions. Avoid omniscient commentary that steps outside character perspective.

Dialogue and Attribution
- Keep dialogue beats short and grounded in action or interior thought. Use simple attribution and actions to anchor who speaks.
