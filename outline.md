DRAFT — outline.md: high-level arc and chapter slots

Overall structure
- Acts: three-act structure. Act I sets stakes and tone; Act II escalates conflict and deepens character; Act III resolves the central arc.

Premise
- Setting: Amity Cove, a small fishing town whose waterfront is being bought up for redevelopment.
- Protagonist: Mara Voss, a former harbor diver now working night dispatch for the harbor patrol under her mentor, Dez Okafor.
- Central conflict: fishing boats have started vanishing near the cove at low tide with no wreckage and no distress calls. Mara is pulled into investigating the pattern, which surfaces both a cover-up tied to Thomas Ridge's waterfront development (which employs her estranged sister, Lena Voss) and the unresolved grief and rift inside her own family.
- Goal: find out what is actually happening to the boats before Ridge's project erases the evidence, and before it costs someone she knows.

Act summaries
- Act I (chapters 1-6): Setup — introduce protagonist, central world, inciting incident, and the primary goal.
- Act II (chapters 7-18): Confrontation — obstacles, reversals, character growth, midpoint complication.
- Act III (chapters 19-24): Resolution — final confrontations, consequences, and a satisfying thematic close.

Major beats (tentative)
- Opening image / status quo
- Inciting incident
- First turning point (doorway to Act II)
- Midpoint (reversal or deepening of stakes)
- Second turning point (begin Act III)
- Climax
- Denouement

Chapter mapping — Act I specs (chapters 01-06)
Each spec is the brief a draft is judged against. Six lines per chapter: FocalCharacter (a name from the STYLE.md Cast), Opens, On-page cast, Required beats, Closing state (what the next chapter inherits), Not yet (what must stay off-page). Names, roles and relationships come from STYLE.md and are not to be redefined in chapter prose. A draft that departs from a line here is not automatically wrong, but the departure must be named in that chapter's ContinuityNotes.

Chapter 01 — Opening image / protagonist and world
- FocalCharacter: Mara Voss.
- Opens: the last hour of a night dispatch shift in the harbor patrol radio room, low tide, the season's first boat overdue.
- On-page cast: Mara Voss; Dez Okafor. Colm Reyes by radio or phone only.
- Required beats: (1) establish Amity Cove, the tide, and Mara's competence at the radio — she reads the water for a living and no longer goes into it; (2) an overdue boat fails to answer and the log shows it is not the first; (3) Dez closes the log himself and tells her to go home, a beat too quickly.
- Closing state: the boat is confirmed missing — no wreckage, no distress call — and Mara has privately decided to look at the earlier log entries.
- Not yet: no Ridge on-page, no Lena on-page, no explanation of the disappearances, no diving.

Chapter 02 — Inciting incident
- FocalCharacter: Mara Voss.
- Opens: daylight on the waterfront the morning after, the town absorbing the loss.
- On-page cast: Mara Voss; Colm Reyes; Dez Okafor briefly.
- Required beats: (1) Colm, who has been watching the tide charts from the bait shop, shows Mara that the disappearances all cluster at the same tide state; (2) Mara asks Dez for the full log and is refused or slow-walked; (3) Mara starts her own record, committing to the investigation.
- Closing state: Mara is investigating without sanction; the tide pattern is on the page as a fact the reader can check.
- Not yet: no proof of a cover-up, no confrontation with Ridge, no reconciliation with Lena.

Chapter 03 — Early attempt and setback
- FocalCharacter: Mara Voss.
- Opens: an attempt to get at the evidence directly — harbor records, a berth, or the water near the cove.
- On-page cast: Mara Voss; Colm Reyes; one harbor or town minor figure (unnamed in the cast, kept minor).
- Required beats: (1) Mara's attempt is competent and nearly works; (2) it is blocked by an ordinary, deniable obstacle — records sealed, an area fenced, a permit cited; (3) the block carries Ridge's project's name on it, which is the first time the development touches the mystery.
- Closing state: Mara has failed cleanly and knows the obstacle is administrative, not natural.
- Not yet: no on-page Ridge, no admission from Dez, no physical danger to Mara.

Chapter 04 — New information complicates the goal
- FocalCharacter: Mara Voss.
- Opens: a meeting Mara did not choose, in a space belonging to the development.
- On-page cast: Mara Voss; Lena Voss; Thomas Ridge (first on-page appearance, brief and courteous).
- Required beats: (1) Mara and Lena's estrangement is shown in behaviour, not summarised; (2) Lena's job makes her the person who controls the record Mara needs; (3) Ridge is polite, helpful and gives Mara something true that makes her problem worse.
- Closing state: the family rift and the mystery are now the same problem; Mara cannot pursue one without damaging the other.
- Not yet: no villain speech from Ridge, no reconciliation, no reveal of what Dez is withholding.

Chapter 05 — Ally and stakes
- FocalCharacter: Colm Reyes (first rotation of focal character; the chapter header must name him, per STYLE.md).
- Opens: the bait shop, before dawn, with another boat due out.
- On-page cast: Colm Reyes; Mara Voss; Dez Okafor.
- Required beats: (1) the town seen from outside Mara's head — what the disappearances cost people who still have to fish; (2) Colm commits to helping Mara, at a cost he names; (3) someone Colm and the reader know is going out on the water anyway.
- Closing state: the stakes are a named person in danger, not an abstraction; Mara has one open ally.
- Not yet: no answer to the mystery, no death on-page.

Chapter 06 — First turning point, doorway to Act II
- FocalCharacter: Mara Voss.
- Opens: the night the boat from Chapter 05 is due back.
- On-page cast: Mara Voss; Dez Okafor; Colm Reyes.
- Required beats: (1) the disappearance happens while Mara is listening, close enough to be real; (2) Dez says or withholds something that proves to Mara he knows more than he has told her; (3) Mara chooses to go back into the water, which is the one thing she has refused since her father's death.
- Closing state: Mara is committed, unsanctioned, and alone with Dez's silence — Act II begins with her diving.
- Not yet: no explanation of the mechanism, no break with Dez, nothing resolved between the sisters.
